Billing and Subscription Support

Subscription Plans:
1. Basic Plan: $29/month
   - Core ESG reporting features
   - Basic analytics
   - Email support

2. Professional Plan: $79/month
   - Advanced ESG reporting
   - Custom dashboards
   - Priority support
   - API access

3. Enterprise Plan: $199/month
   - Full platform access
   - Custom integrations
   - Dedicated support
   - Advanced analytics

Payment Information:
- We accept all major credit cards
- Payments are processed securely
- Invoices are sent monthly
- Payment history is available in your account

Billing Policies:
- Subscriptions are billed monthly
- You can upgrade or downgrade at any time
- Cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period
- Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase

Common Billing Issues:
1. Payment Declined:
   - Check card expiration date
   - Verify billing address
   - Ensure sufficient funds
   - Contact your bank if needed

2. Subscription Changes:
   - Changes take effect immediately
   - Prorated charges may apply
   - Downgrades don't affect current month

3. Invoice Requests:
   - Download from your account dashboard
   - Contact support for custom invoices
   - Tax information is included

4. Cancellation:
   - Cancel anytime from your account
   - Access continues until period ends
   - No cancellation fees

For billing support, please provide:
- Your account email
- Transaction ID (if applicable)
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots if relevant
