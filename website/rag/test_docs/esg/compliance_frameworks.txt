ESG Compliance Frameworks and Standards

Major ESG Reporting Frameworks:

1. Global Reporting Initiative (GRI):
   - Most widely used sustainability reporting framework
   - Provides comprehensive guidelines for ESG reporting
   - Covers economic, environmental, and social impacts
   - Includes sector-specific standards
   - Used by over 10,000 organizations worldwide

2. Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB):
   - Industry-specific ESG standards
   - Focuses on financially material sustainability information
   - 77 industry-specific standards
   - Designed for SEC filings and financial reporting
   - Merged with ISSB in 2022

3. Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD):
   - Focuses on climate-related financial risks
   - Four pillars: Governance, Strategy, Risk Management, Metrics & Targets
   - Widely adopted by financial institutions
   - Mandatory in some jurisdictions

4. Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP):
   - Global environmental disclosure system
   - Focuses on climate change, water security, and forests
   - Used by investors and companies
   - Annual reporting cycle

5. International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB):
   - New global sustainability standards
   - IFRS S1 (General Requirements) and IFRS S2 (Climate)
   - Designed for global capital markets
   - Builds on existing frameworks

Compliance Requirements:
- Many jurisdictions now require ESG reporting
- Financial institutions increasingly demand ESG data
- Investors use ESG information for decision-making
- Regulatory requirements vary by region and industry

Best Practices:
- Start with materiality assessment
- Align with multiple frameworks
- Ensure data quality and accuracy
- Regular stakeholder engagement
- Continuous improvement process
